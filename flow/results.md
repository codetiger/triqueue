# Physical design results

Generated by `flow/results.py` from the run records in `flow/records/`. Do not edit by hand.

Post-route results of `tq_top` from OpenROAD-flow-scripts (`flow/ppa.py`), with power from simulated switching activity of each workload (`flow/power.py`). How each number is produced: `flow/README.md`.

- **Engine only.** The operand SRAM is outside `tq_top`; its area and energy are not included.
- **Cell area** is the placed standard-cell area (logic, flops, clock tree, buffers, taps), without fill. kGE = cell area ÷ the PDK's NAND2 area.
- **Fmax** is from post-route STA at the target clock (1 ÷ (period − worst slack)). Peak TOPS/mm² = 2 × T² × Fmax ÷ cell area. Energy is measured at the target clock, including where post-route timing misses it by a few percent (negative slack).
- **Workloads** run in Verilator against the cycle model; operands are uniform random int8, more switching than real data. Energy and utilization cover the whole simulated window (the run plus about 20 setup cycles), and energy is charged to the useful MACs (M × N × K).
