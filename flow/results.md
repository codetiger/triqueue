# Physical design results

Generated by `flow/results.py` from the run records in `flow/records/`. Do not edit by hand.

Post-route results of `tq_top` from OpenROAD-flow-scripts (`flow/ppa.py`), with power from simulated switching activity of each workload (`flow/power.py`). How each number is produced: `flow/README.md`.

- **Engine only** unless a table says otherwise. The operand SRAM is outside `tq_top`; where an open macro with characterized power exists (sky130hd), a separate table adds its access energy.
- **Cell area** is the placed standard-cell area (logic, flops, clock tree, buffers, taps), without fill. kGE = cell area ÷ the PDK's NAND2 area.
- **Fmax** is from post-route STA at the target clock (1 ÷ (period − worst slack)). Peak TOPS/mm² = 2 × T² × Fmax ÷ cell area. Energy is measured at the target clock, including where post-route timing misses it by a few percent (negative slack).
- **Workloads** run in Verilator against the cycle model; operands are uniform random int8, more switching than real data. Energy and utilization cover the whole simulated window (the run plus about 20 setup cycles), and energy is charged to the useful MACs (M × N × K). `*_relu` workloads repeat a shape with synthetic inference-like operands (post-ReLU uint8 activations, about half zeros, and Gaussian int8 weights; not data from a real network) and are left out of the geometric means.

Energy per access 179.1 pJ, from the OpenRAM liberty (sky130_sram_1rw1r_128x256_8_TT_1p8V_25C.lib): OpenRAM gives the same energy for every clock edge whether the macro reads, writes or is idle, so each access is charged one clock cycle (SRAM clock gated when idle) plus leakage. Access counts are the workload's read and write transactions. Capacity does not fit the workloads; this is the energy of the port's traffic.
